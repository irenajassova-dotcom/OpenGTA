SENEC '98 : ZVUK ULICE
======================

A fan-made mission pack set in Senec (Slovakia) during the birth of Slovak
hip hop. Tribute only: the mission events are fictional and not affiliated
with or endorsed by any of the artists mentioned.

Background (real history)
-------------------------
* Dávid zo Senca rapped in Slovak from 1989 and is called the forefather
  ("praotec") of Slovak hip hop. Geto Gang - "Politik" (1993) is one of the
  first Slovak rap recordings.
* His compilation project "Zvuk ulice" (Street Sound) gathered young MCs
  from all over Slovakia in the 1990s.
* Rytmus first appeared on Zvuk ulice as "Pa3k Metamorfolord". In 2001 he
  founded Kontrafakt with Ego and DJ Anys. The hit "Dáva mi" and the album
  E.R.A. (2003-2004) took Slovak rap into the mainstream.

Missions
--------
 1/5 Prvá sloka           buy a blank TDK tape at the market, record a verse
 2/5 Zvuk ulice           deliver demo tapes: station, Turecký dom, lake bufet
 3/5 Metamorfolord        pick up Pa3k from the train, hold the beat at the session
 4/5 Steny na jazerách    tag garages, underpass and pier, lose the police
 5/5 Krst kompilácie      fetch the tapes from the pressing plant, perform at the lakes

Two ways to play
----------------
1) Browser (no game data needed): open web/senec98/index.html.
   A self-contained top-down port with a hand-built Senec map (sídlisko,
   Námestie 1. mája with Turecký dom, the station, Slnečné jazerá, D1),
   traffic, pedestrians, police, a pager, a synthesized boom-bap car radio
   and a rhythm mini-game for recording sessions.

2) Native OpenGTA engine (needs the original GTA1 data files and a Lua 5.x
   build, see BUILD):
       ./viewer -s scripts/senec98.lua
   This uses two new Lua bindings added in lua_addon/lua_player.cpp:
       player.getPos() getCash() addCash(n) getWanted() setWanted(n)
              addWanted(n) getLives() giveLives(n)
       hud.setTitle(s) setObjective(s) pager(s [,secs]) clear()
   The HUD lines are drawn by viewer.cpp. Because the GTA1 maps are not
   Senec, the waypoints are block offsets from the player's start position.
